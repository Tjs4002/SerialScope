# Code of conduct

SerialScope is a small, friendly project. Everyone taking part, whether in issues, pull requests or discussions, is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to electronics or programming.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before assuming the worst.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Tjs4002).
